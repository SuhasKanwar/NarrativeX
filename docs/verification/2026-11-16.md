## Failures found and corrected

- The configured NVIDIA Super model returned HTTP 404 on this account, despite appearing in the model catalog. Ultra returned HTTP 200 and now serves planning, analysis, and judging. Groq remains restricted to `openai/gpt-oss-120b`.
- The NVIDIA SDK's automatic structured-output fallback sent an unsupported `guided_json` parameter. Evaluation now explicitly calls a named schema tool and validates its arguments with Pydantic. Missing or wrongly named tool results fail validation.
- Optional excerpt fields allowed the model to omit comparison evidence. Those fields are now required in the generated schema; empty evidence produces an insufficient-evidence result.
- Comparison searches ordered by publication time returned poorly aligned news. The planner now prefers relevance unless the user requests latest coverage.
- Ctrl+Enter did not insert a newline in Chromium. The composer now explicitly inserts one at the selection while preserving Enter submission and input-method composition.

## Actual research result

The browser submitted: “Compare relevant news and social coverage of AI regulation using relevancy ordering. Focus on two shared themes with exact excerpts.”

The completed run used NVIDIA Ultra for evaluation and `nvidia/nemotron-3-embed-1b` for query and claim embeddings. It generated 19 nodes, 16 directed edges, and 11 finding excerpts. A separate check against the saved source snapshot confirmed that every retained finding excerpt occurred verbatim in its cited document. This check establishes attribution, not semantic entailment.

The result retained a partially-agree comparison on industry influence and regulatory gaps, with one news citation and two social citations. Another comparison lacked retained news excerpts and was marked insufficient evidence. The UI displayed the comparison matrix, evidence ledger, graph, verification tasks, mathematical metrics, and judge assessment.

The internal quality composite was 0.6092, claim citation validity 1.0, and comparison citation validity 0.5. These diagnostics expose the sample's limitations; they are not factual accuracy scores. A previous failed attempt was preserved as a source-only fallback rather than presented as a successful assessment.
